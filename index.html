Projects

AgentOps Guardian

International AI Builders Congress 2026 · Solo

A control layer that stops runaway AI agents and lets each agent earn its permissions, like a credit score for AI.

I built Guardian for the International AI Builders Congress AgentOps challenge. It sits between an AI agent and its tools, where it can stop repeated or over-budget runs and give the operator a way to pause or approve actions. Its trust-score idea is an experiment in connecting permissions with observed behaviour.

  • Circuit breaker for exact repeats, cycles, semantic loops, step limits and budget overruns
  • Trust score from 0 to 100 with four tiers that decide what runs without human approval
  • Injection shield that scans tool results for hidden instructions
  • Pause, kill, approve and override from a dashboard, REST API or MCP server

BGC Bus Tracker v2

Versity Tech Fest 2026 · Solo

Real-time campus bus tracking with a live map, one-tap driver location sharing and a mobile-first interface.

I made this for students who have no easy way to tell whether a campus bus is close or delayed. In the prototype, a driver can share a GPS location and students can view the four buses, their status and route information on a map.

  • Live or offline status for all four buses, with last update time
  • Bottom sheet showing route stops and schedule

DermaSheba (ডার্মাসেবা)

2025 to 2026 · Solo

An AI skin-condition awareness tool that reads a photo and gives urgency guidance in English and Bangla.

DermaSheba is an awareness project exploring how image classification and Bangla explanations might help someone decide what to do next. It is a prototype, not a medical service, and its output should not be treated as a diagnosis.

  • Instant image classification for JPG and PNG uploads
  • Recognises 7 skin conditions, including melanoma and basal cell carcinoma
  • High, medium and low urgency, each with a clear next step
  • States plainly that it supports awareness and is not a diagnosis

LungSheba

SciBlitz AI Challenge 2026 · Team project

A chest X-ray awareness prototype that presents model results with urgency labels and Bangla explanations.

Research interests

A question I keep exploring

What could an intelligent system remember about a person, and what would that mean?

I’m still learning about this area. I’m interested in agentic AI, cognitive computing and systems that use memory to make interactions more personal over time.

What interests me: whether parts of a person’s knowledge, stories and preferences could be represented in a computational system, and how such a system might change through interaction.

What needs care: questions of consent, privacy, identity and the limits of what a model can represent. I’d like to study the technical and ethical foundations before making strong claims about what these systems can do.
